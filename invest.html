What We Proved

People choose
KOKOS for the taste.

Nine years in, we've earned loyal customer demand, run scoop shops, developed proprietary plant-based recipes, and learned what it takes to operate a food business people genuinely love — without spending a dollar on advertising.

That's our proof: the recipes, the standards, the demand, the regulars who keep coming back. Everything ahead of us rests on it.

Where We're Going

Bigger than
one shop.

KOKOS showed us what we're capable of at the scale of one storefront. Emerald Sea exists to put that same product in far more hands — more cities, more shelves, more people who never had a dessert they could actually eat.

We're raising capital now to fund that step. What you'd be investing in is the whole picture — our shops, wholesale accounts, grocery and retail, events, and direct-to-customer — all under one company.
