We started with a simple belief: allergy-friendly dessert should never feel like a compromise. Since then we've built loyal customer demand, operated scoop shops, developed proprietary plant-based recipes, and learned what it takes to run a food business that people genuinely love — without spending a dollar on advertising.

The next phase isn't about abandoning what worked. It's about rebuilding around the strongest parts of KOKOS: the product, the brand, and the people who believe in it.
