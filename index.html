Our story

Born from a
love of ice cream

Sam Brooker and Jerusa van Lith didn't plan to become ice cream disruptors — they were deep in Nashville's music industry. Sam can't eat dairy, so what started as a search for better options became an obsession. In 2019 they launched KOKOS (Dutch for coconut, a nod to Jerusa's Amsterdam roots) and built a devoted following entirely through word of mouth — not a single dollar spent on paid marketing.
