Our Story

From music
to ice cream

Sam Brooker and Jerusa van Lith didn't plan to become ice cream disruptors — they were deep in Nashville's music industry. Sam can't eat dairy, so what started as a search for better options became a full-blown obsession. In 2019 they launched KOKOS (Dutch for coconut, a nod to Jerusa's Amsterdam roots) and built a devoted following — entirely through word of mouth. Not a single dollar spent on paid marketing. Nine years strong.

Step into the flagship at OneC1TY and you feel it immediately — emerald velvet seating, warm lighting, happy plants, and pale ice-cream-colored walls. Mirrors everywhere so you feel good about treating yourself. No guilt. Just joy.

The Secret

Objectively
better

The base is 100% organic coconut milk and cream, organic sugar, pink Himalayan salt, premium chocolate, and fresh fruit. No artificial dyes, no shortcuts. European gelato silkiness meets classic American ice cream richness. People walk in skeptical, take one bite, and say the same thing: "Wait — this is plant-based?"
