About SKOOP
The KOKOS Token

Your skoop,
on-chain.

SKOOP is the loyalty and payment token for KOKOS Ice Cream — Nashville's original plant-based scoop shop. Built on Base, it lets you buy tokens, pay at the register, and earn rewards just for being a regular. No points cards. No apps. Just your web3 wallet.

Every SKOOP transaction at the shop flows through an on-chain escrow and settles transparently — no middlemen, no processing fees eating into your purchase. It's the first crypto-native checkout experience in Nashville's food scene.

How It Works

Three ways
to use SKOOP

1
Get SKOOP
Connect your wallet and tap Buy. Swap USDC or ETH for SKOOP directly through Uniswap V3 on Base — no account required, no KYC.
2
Pay at the shop
The KOKOS team rings up your order and shows you a checkout QR. Open the Pay tab, scan it, review the total, and confirm — SKOOP goes to escrow instantly.
3
Earn rewards
Paying with Square or cash? Show your wallet QR to the KOKOS team — they'll send SKOOP to your wallet. Paid with SKOOP? Rewards are sent to you automatically — no QR needed.
4
Settlement & burn
SKOOP captured in escrow settles on-chain and a portion burns — reducing supply over time. Every scoop you buy makes the token a little more scarce.
